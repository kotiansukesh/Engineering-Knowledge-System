# Architecture Governance

Governance should improve decision quality without becoming a central bottleneck.

## Mechanisms

- architecture principles;
- lightweight review thresholds;
- ADRs;
- reference architectures;
- risk registers;
- exception process;
- measurable fitness functions.

## Review trigger

Increase governance when repeated architectural failures show a systemic control gap; reduce it when governance adds delay without reducing risk.
