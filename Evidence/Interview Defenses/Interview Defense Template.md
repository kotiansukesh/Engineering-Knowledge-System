# Interview Defense

## Explain

State the problem and mechanism without notes.

## Defend

State the constraints, decision and trade-offs.

## Challenge

Change one constraint and identify what breaks.

## Redesign

Describe the smallest architecture or implementation change that restores the required behavior.

## Evidence

Link implementation, benchmark, failure experiment or ADR.

## Reflection

What evidence would change the decision?
