# Blind Practice

> Solve without opening the pattern note. The objective is recognition under uncertainty.

## Attempt protocol

Before looking at a solution:

- Pattern candidate
- Why this candidate
- Brute force
- Constraint that breaks brute force
- Invariant
- Complexity target
- Confidence 1–5
- What fooled me

After solving:

- Actual pattern
- First incorrect assumption
- Alternative valid approach
- Edge case missed
- Review date

## Recognition scoring

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Immediate recognition + invariant |
| 4 | Correct after brief exploration |
| 3 | Correct after deriving brute force |
| 2 | Correct after a hint |
| 1 | Pattern missed |

## Mastery gate

Move a pattern to Mastered only after:

- 2 unseen recognition attempts;
- invariant explained without notes;
- template written from memory;
- complexity explained;
- one non-applicable case explained;
- one variation or combination solved.
